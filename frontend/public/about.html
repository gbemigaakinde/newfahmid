About Fahmid School

Who we are, where we started, and what we believe in.

Our Mission

To be the first in providing learning and creativity in the hands of highly trained, loving, caring, and God-fearing teachers in a fun-filled environment.

Our Vision

To be the most referred home of learning celebrated for academic and moral excellence in Africa.

Our Story

From a Tutorial Centre to a Celebrated School

In 2006, the school started out as a small tutorial centre for secondary school students. The founder was working a regular job at the time but used every free moment — after work, during short breaks — to sit with students, go over what they were struggling with, and help them catch up.

Word spread. Results improved. Parents started talking, and the centre picked up a name: Multiple Success Tutorial. Then something unexpected happened — parents of much younger children, toddlers really, began asking if their little ones could also attend during the holiday coaching periods.

Running a full nursery and primary school was never the original plan. The turning point came when the founder was brought in to manage an existing school that was going through serious problems. Despite genuine efforts to turn things around, progress was slow and the situation made it clear that a fresh start was the only real option.

In September 2007, a new site was found and the school opened on its own terms. It started as Multiple Success Academy, later became Fahmid Kiddies Academy, and eventually grew into what it is today — Fahmid Nursery and Primary School.

Through all of that, one thing has never changed: the belief that children deserve more than just good grades. They deserve teachers who genuinely care, a school where they feel safe, and an environment that helps them grow in character as much as in knowledge.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These are the things we actually care about — not just words on a wall, but how we run the school every day.

Love & Care

Every child who walks through our gate is looked after properly — not just academically, but as a person. Our teachers know their pupils by name and by nature.

Respect for Each Child

No two children are the same. We treat each pupil as an individual — with their own pace, their own strengths, and their own way of making sense of the world.

A Love for Learning

We want children to enjoy coming to school — to be curious, to ask questions, and to leave each day knowing something they didn't know when they arrived.

Integrity

We teach children to do the right thing — whether someone is watching or not. Honesty and good character matter just as much to us as test scores.

Discipline

Good behaviour is not something we demand — it's something we build. Children learn to be responsible and take school seriously because they understand why it matters.

Teamwork

We encourage children to work together, support each other, and share credit. These habits — built early — stay with them for life.

Our Campus

Our Facilities

Clean, safe spaces set up specifically for how young children learn and play.

Bright Classrooms

Spacious, well-lit rooms with the right furniture and learning materials for each age group. Nothing cramped, nothing cluttered.

Library

A good selection of age-appropriate books that children can actually sit with and enjoy — building reading habits one page at a time.

Play Areas

Outdoor and indoor spaces where children can run around, use equipment, and just be kids. Play is part of learning here, not an afterthought.

Secure Premises

The gate is controlled, entrances are watched, and children are supervised throughout the school day. Parents drop off knowing their child is in safe hands.

Ministry of Education Accredited

Fahmid Nursery and Primary School is fully registered and approved by the Ministry of Education. We meet all the required standards for teaching quality and child safety — so parents know exactly what they're signing up to.
