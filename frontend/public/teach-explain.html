Enter Results

Record CA breakdown scores and exam scores for every pupil, then submit for admin approval

Important: Pupils cannot see results until admin approves them.
Saving a result stores it as a private draft. Only after you press "Submit for Approval" and admin approves it will pupils and parents see the scores on their report cards.

What the status banners mean

Pending Admin Approval

You have submitted, and the admin has not yet reviewed. Inputs are locked — you cannot edit until admin acts.

Results Rejected

Admin found an error and sent the results back. A reason is shown in red. Inputs become editable again so you can fix and resubmit.

Results Locked / Approved

Admin has approved and published the results. Pupils can now see them. You can no longer edit. Contact admin to unlock if a genuine correction is needed.

Direct Publish (if enabled)

Some teacher accounts have "direct publish" permission. If yours does, the Submit button publishes results immediately — a confirmation dialog always appears first.

Good practice: Save your scores after every few pupils using "Save All Results". Only submit when the entire class's scores for that subject are complete and checked.

Manual Totals tab

Switch to the Manual Totals tab when you need to enter attendance totals directly. Each pupil gets three number boxes:

Times School Opened

Total number of days school was in session during this term.

Times Present

How many of those days the pupil was physically in school.

Times Absent

How many days the pupil was not present.

Validation rules are enforced: Times Present cannot exceed Times School Opened. Times Absent cannot exceed Times School Opened. Present + Absent cannot exceed Times School Opened. The system will highlight any errors in red and prevent saving until they are fixed.

Daily Register overwrites Manual Totals. When you use the Daily Register tab, it automatically recalculates and overwrites the Manual Totals for each pupil. Use Manual Totals only for terms where you did not use the daily register.

CBT — Online Tests & Exams

Create and manage objective tests that pupils take digitally on the pupil portal

CBT stands for Computer-Based Testing. You create a test here with multiple-choice questions. The test appears in the pupil portal where pupils answer it online. Their scores are recorded automatically — no marking required.

Create a new test

Press + Create New Test. A form opens with two sections: Test Details and Questions.

3

Add questions

Press + Add Question. A question block appears with a text area for the question, four option fields: A, B, C, D (C and D are optional), and a Correct radio button next to each option — click the one that is correct.

Press + Add Question again to add more. You can remove any question with the X button in its top-right corner. There is no limit on the number of questions.

4

Save as draft or publish immediately

Save as Draft stores the test but keeps it hidden from pupils. Save & Publish makes it immediately visible to pupils in the selected class.

5

View results

From the test list, press Results on any test. You see a table of every pupil who submitted, their score, percentage, grade, number of tab-switches (anti-cheat), and submission time.

Tab switches are flagged in red if a pupil switched away from the test window more than 3 times — this may indicate they were looking at another source during the test.

6

Delete a test

Press Delete. A full-screen confirmation dialog appears warning that all questions, all pupil scores, and all submission records will be permanently deleted. You must confirm to proceed. This cannot be undone.

CBT scores are separate from CA/Exam scores. A CBT test result does not automatically feed into the Enter Results section. CBT is for online practice and assessment — if you want CBT scores to appear on report cards, you must manually enter them in Enter Results.

Quick Reference — Common Questions

Fast answers to the most frequent questions

Why can't I see certain subjects in Enter Results?

Subjects are assigned to your class by admin. If a subject is missing from the dropdown, contact admin to add it to your class.

I saved results but they disappeared — what happened?

Results are saved as drafts under your account and should persist. Make sure you selected the correct term, class, and subject combination.

Why are the result inputs greyed out?

Results are locked — either you have submitted and admin is reviewing, or admin has approved and locked them. Check the banner at the bottom of the results section for details.

Why don't remarks suggestions appear?

Suggestions are generated from approved results. If admin has not yet approved results for the selected term, suggestions cannot be generated.

Can I edit an already-submitted lesson note?

No. Once submitted, a note is locked until admin acts. If admin approves it, it stays locked. If admin rejects it, it reverts to Draft and you can edit.

How does the daily attendance affect the report card?

When you use the daily register, the system automatically calculates "Times School Opened", "Times Present", and "Times Absent" for each pupil — these appear on the report card.

The portal says "No classes assigned" — what do I do?

Contact admin. They need to assign you to a class before you can enter any data. Once assigned, refresh the portal page and the class will appear.

How do I correct a mistake after results are approved?

You cannot edit locked results yourself. Contact admin and explain the error. Admin can unlock the results, allowing you to edit and resubmit.
