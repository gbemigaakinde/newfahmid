Admissions

How to get your child a place at Fahmid School — and what to expect along the way.

Enrolment Open Admissions for the 2026–2027 Academic Year are now open. Places are limited. Apply Today

How It Works

The Admissions Process

Five straightforward steps from first enquiry to your child's first day.

1

Get in Touch

Fill in the enquiry form below or call us directly. Either way works.

2

Visit the School

Come and see the school in person, meet the staff, and have a look around.

3

Assessment

A short, relaxed assessment suited to your child's age — nothing to stress about.

4

Offer of Place

If a space is available, we send you a formal offer in writing.

5

Complete Enrolment

Submit the required documents, pay the registration fee, and your child is in.

What You'll Need

Year Groups & Entry Requirements

Nursery 1

Ages 2 – 3

  • Birth certificate
  • Immunisation record
  • Passport photograph (×4)
  • Parent/guardian ID
Nursery 2 & KG

Ages 3 – 5

  • All Nursery 1 documents
  • Previous school report (if any)
  • Informal readiness check
Primary 1 – 6

Ages 6 – 12

  • Birth certificate
  • Last two school reports
  • Passport photographs (×4)
  • Short entry test (English & Maths)
